# Studies

[Study 01 — Corpus, evidence map, panel and codebook](01-corpus/README.md) establishes
the populations, evidence coverage, production taxonomy, panel and shared codebook.
Its four-film calibration and all sixteen panel case files are complete; prompt
labels at scale are the next slice, in the order set by [PROGRAMME.md](../PROGRAMME.md).

Use studies/NN-slug/ for each study, with its README.md report, queries/code, and
derived data under outputs/. Large derived data belongs in release assets, never
git. Apply the programme's evidence labels and full nine-item output contract.

[Panel case files — complete index](../case-files/README.md) records all sixteen
registered films across all 49 shared codes for Studies 02–10, with scoped evidence and reproducible profiles.

[Provisional prompt-label infrastructure — AF3a](../prompt-labels/README.md) supplies structural features, the balanced sample, single-agent gold and the local labelling/agreement harness; the real model run and assessment follow separately.

[Study 10 — Organisation and production economics](10-organisation/README.md) measures public workspace organisation, author and owner records, event windows, repetition, placements and raw input reuse across 157 primary histories, with a resource-visibility audit and proposed operating requirements; labour, costs and comparable-format productivity remain unmeasurable.

[Study 02 — Planning and production decomposition](02-planning/README.md) measures structural planning evidence across 157 histories, traces all 16 panel maps, separates declarations from local dependencies, and proposes brief, scene, shot and task schemas with explicit evidence limits.

[Study 03 — Reusable elements](03-elements/README.md) measures disclosed elements, attachments and structural preservation evidence across 157 histories, traces panel packages and inferred derivatives, and proposes separate element identity, variants, versions, scoped authority and approval with explicit evidence limits.

[Study 05 — Image construction and editing](05-image/README.md) measures image bindings, conservative operation routes, construction edges and structural edit/preservation wording across 155 image-bearing histories; it traces panel recipes, separates requests from masks/layers and outcomes, and proposes operation contracts with explicit dependencies on Study 04 and later studies.

[Study 06 — Video generation and shot control](06-video/README.md) measures recorded input routes, literal model/version controls and structural shot wording across 156 video-bearing histories; it traces motion and revision requests, separates instructions from outcomes, and proposes a video-node specification with explicit Study 04 and later-study dependencies.

[Study 07 — Lineage and cross-shot reuse](07-lineage/README.md): typed disclosed graphs, shared-source/chaining comparisons, all sixteen panel maps and a proposed invalidation policy.

[Study 08 — Model roles and workflow](08-workflow/README.md): literal model/operation and scoped semantic role matrices, explicit model transitions versus co-occurrence, overlapping workflow archetypes, traced handoffs and proposed node contracts across 157 primary histories; Study 04 semantic refinement remains pending.

[Study 09 — Iteration](09-iteration/README.md): overlapping candidate-family rules, prompt/input/control comparisons and further-work adoption across 157 primary histories; traced revision histories, an iteration taxonomy and proposed review states with bounded retry, while semantic refinement waits on Study 04.
