Provisional AF3a v1 structural features and semantic sample source IDs, built from
the verified data-2026-10-20 snapshot under Study 01 codebook 1.0.

Assets: features.parquet (61,542 exact-text rows), occurrences.parquet (160,753
compound source-field occurrences), sample.jsonl.gz (5,000 unique primary-film
text IDs), SHA256SUMS (all three asset hashes). No full prompt texts. The sample
excludes texts above 12,000 characters; structural features retain them.

Method, exclusions, gold limits and commands: prompt-labels/README.md in the
release's source commit. Labels and semantic prevalence are provisional; the
local model run and agreement assessment follow in a later slice. This does not
alter or relicense the original third-party data.
