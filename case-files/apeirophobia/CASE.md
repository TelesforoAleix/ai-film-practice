Film `c3d8ee5e-b119-4654-99f9-c6545f650635`, primary, both cohort groups.
Codebook 1.0: **43 coded, six unknown**, 49 assignments.
[Full evidence ledger](codes.csv); [full-record profile and 63-job reading register](profile.json).
All 6,862 jobs and all film-scoped rows in all 14 tables were profiled. The
purposive 63-job technical reading covers every model/type, populated folder,
input-role-set and selected control/transition stratum; the exact algorithm,
limitations and seven folders without generation placements are in the
[index](../README.md). The whole folder register and one disclosed reference
record were inspected. The brief is unavailable. Sample absence remains unknown.
This selected case cannot establish what separates top films from other films.

planning links without constructing a missing master plan from folders or dates.

## Study 04 — Prompt and context

**Observed:** the tattoo request names numbered image tokens and has two actual
input asset IDs (04.1–04.5). It says “dont change image <<<image_1>>> only the
tattoo”; the preservation property is explicit in text, while attached media
contents remain unknown. **Interpretation:** this is a short edit request;
neither achieved identity nor a complete context specification is observed.
No sufficiently scoped conflicting pair is established in the sample (04.6
unknown). **Proposed:** retain the edit target, preservation span and actual input
bindings; do not fill media properties from attachment presence.

## Study 05 — Image construction

**Observed:** the same tattoo task has local-edit and multi-input intent
(05.1–05.3). A separate `seedream_v5_pro` cover request,
`01ece7aa-3624-409c-bd67-a09e29240c38`, explicitly has `is_inpaint=false` (05.4).
`02fb03d9-dc28-447f-8641-7393505f3aa4` explicitly requests a festival key-art

control does not describe the tattoo edit's mechanism. Multiple conditioning
inputs do not prove layers or a successful composite. **Proposed:** preserve
operation intent, purpose and mechanism with their own generation IDs.

with task-specific controls and no assumed cross-model equivalence.

## Study 07 — Lineage

**Observed:** `2141cb13-6107-48ed-aa6a-80dcaec6ec53` produces the video input used
by the named belt-edit request (07.1, 07.4). The profile contains 18,662 distinct
producer/consumer/asset/role/path tuples across 28,411 usage occurrences; these
are edges, not independent assets or a graph-degree ranking. Three named cover
requests share four exact input IDs (07.2), while explicitly grouped raw/min
metadata remains within one asset entity and cross-ID aliases are unresolved
(07.3). **Interpretation:** scoped motifs are supportable without reconstructing
all semantic identity or canonical-reference relationships.
**Proposed:** changing `88160a68-3dd4-4662-be7e-4fb5852634c5` creates a two-generation
review set: `b6e44e77-d4a0-4821-9663-55a452fffc01` and
`311a3f4c-be35-4c70-8d8e-2b882804d9cd`, searching all exported edges (07.5).
Reachability does not prove visual impact or include hidden dependencies.

## Study 09 — Iteration

**Observed:** cover requests `01ece7aa-3624-409c-bd67-a09e29240c38`,
`0a8dca0d-a050-4d30-ba25-81fbd9209ef3` and
`123d45e6-1d2d-45d7-8154-de23704b7d55` share identical primary text and four input
IDs across two literal models (09.1). The tattoo text independently establishes
local-correction intent (09.2). The producer/video-edit input edge establishes
adoption for further work (09.4). **Interpretation:** shared inputs and text do
not settle directional revision order or a codeable revision delta (09.3 unknown),
and reuse is not approval or proof that a prior output failed.
**Proposed:** group candidates while keeping corrections, deltas, reuse and
acceptance as distinct evidence-backed events.

## Study 10 — Organisation and resources

**Observed:** the 27-folder register mixes entity, place, act/image/video and
Audio labels (10.1, inferred organisation). All 6,862 jobs have author IDs and
parseable timestamps; two authors are recorded, with UTC extrema 2026-08-17
07:09:12.706883 to 2026-09-12 18:15:13.986484 (10.2–10.3). There are 6,858 nonempty
primary fields, 1,689 distinct nonempty texts and 5,169 repeat occurrences beyond
the first; 83/6,862 generations have multiple folder placements (10.4). Four
primary fields are explicitly empty (profile). The proxy is 6,862 jobs /
(542.907007 catalogue-declared seconds / 60) = **758.361920 jobs per declared
minute** (10.5). **Interpretation:** two authors do not establish team structure;
Audio is a label, not sound-production evidence. Repetition is not waste and the
proxy is not cost, labour, speed or productivity. **Proposed:** show per-film
counts with their capture and duration denominators, retaining placement/entity
and author/owner distinctions.

Unknowns are brief intent/template classification, actual build order, element
versions, a sufficiently scoped context conflict and directional revision delta.
Later studies should reuse these named examples and whole-record profiles without
turning this purposive sample into a semantic census. All proposals await the
tests in the [case-file index](../README.md).
